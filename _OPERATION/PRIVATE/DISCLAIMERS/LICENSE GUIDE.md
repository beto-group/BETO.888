### **BETO.GROUP Software Release License Guide (MIT License)**

Welcome! This guide explains the simple and permissive licensing for BETO.GROUP software Releases (the Obsidian vaults we provide). Our goal is to make our work as easy as possible for you to use, build upon, and share.

**Understanding this guide is key to using our Releases correctly, especially if you create your own projects ("Derivative Works") based on them.**

### 1. Our Simple License: The MIT License

All BETO.GROUP software Releases are provided to you under the **MIT License**.

This is a single, straightforward license that never changes or expires. There are no complex time windows or alternative license paths.

**What the MIT License Lets You Do:**  
The MIT License is one of the most permissive open-source licenses. It gives you broad freedom to:

- **Use:** Freely use the software for any purpose (personal, educational, commercial).
- **Copy & Distribute:** Make and share copies of the software.
- **Modify:** Change the software to suit your needs or create new things from it.
- **Sublicense & Sell:** You can even include our work in a commercial product you sell.


**Your Single Core Responsibility:**  
The only major requirement is that you must include the original **copyright notice and the permission notice (the full text of the MIT License)** in any copy or substantial portion of the software you distribute.

### 2. Building Your Own Projects (Derivative Works)

This is where the simplicity of the MIT License shines! "Derivative Works" are new projects or customized vaults you create that use parts of one or more BETO.GROUP Releases.

Because all our components are under the MIT License, you have maximum flexibility.
- You can use our components in your own projects, whether those projects are open-source or proprietary.
- You can license your own original additions under any terms you choose.
- The only thing you **must** do is preserve the BETO.GROUP copyright and MIT License text for the parts you use from our Releases.
