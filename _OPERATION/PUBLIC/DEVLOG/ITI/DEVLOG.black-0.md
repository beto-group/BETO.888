The initial release of the BETO.GROUP Obsidian Vault was a massive undertaking. Our original plan was to launch with a vast, curated knowledge base called the "Enigmas" module. However, based on invaluable early feedback that the vault's structure was overwhelming, we made a critical decision to pivot.

We postponed the "Enigmas" content to focus on a more approachable introduction. Instead, we developed a mini-game designed to familiarize users with the unique architecture and systems we use across our entire vault ecosystem. This hands-on approach proved to be a far better onboarding experience.

#### Key Features & Deliverables

- **Onboarding Mini-Game:** A new, interactive experience created to introduce users to our vault's core architectural concepts in a hands-on way.
- **Tooling - "Datacore" Components:** A foundational set of Obsidian-native tools, templates, and frameworks that power the vault.
- **Resources - Asset Library:** A core collection of visual assets for use within user projects.
- **Legal Framework:** Deployed our first version of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, including a mechanism to ensure user agreement before vault access.

#### Known Issues & Limitations

Transparency is a core value. Here are the known issues at the time of this release:

- **Aquarium Canvas:** Potential rendering issues with `13 Aquarium {FireStormFrontier 🫡}`.
- **Canvas Instability (CRITICAL):** Adding the "World888" node to a canvas may cause instability. Attempting to remove it can crash the canvas entirely. Please avoid this interaction.
- **ContentExplorer Navigation:** The "back" button functionality is non-operational.
- **Kanban Board:** The implementation is preliminary and not recommended for critical task management.
- **Globe/Map View:** The "Globe" component has limited functionality; a full map view would require a major architectural redesign.
