

January was… a trip. We started the month focusing on recovery, taking a sustainable approach to the work. But by the end? We were in pure grind mode.

We’ve been thinking hard about self-sufficiency this year. The goal is clear, but the workflow was the bottleneck. We realized that if we want to make our product releases bigger and better, we can’t just build in silence.

**The Shift: 1000x Marketing**  
We found some massive inspiration this month. We realized we need to stop simply "creating a garden no one can see." The vision is there for those who want to dig, but our focus needs to shift heavily toward marketing and content.

**The Tech Win**  
The speed at which we can validate a new Datacore component has dropped from **a few days to just a couple of hours**.

- **Old Goal:** Make it work.
- **New Goal:** Bridge the gap from Theory to Production in max one day.


This speed allows us to focus on the output—the content—rather than getting stuck in the weeds of development.

**Status Update: Marketplace & Beto.888**  
We made the call to push these back. Inspiration comes first. If we had tried to bruteforce these projects earlier in the month, we would have realized halfway through that we were doing it inefficiently. The new insights we have now will let us build them better and faster later. Better to wait for the right approach than to polish the wrong thing.

It’s not always the most obvious path, but we love the process. We gotta do what we must.

We'll be around.. 🫡
