
>[!danger]- ###### I. Monumental Overhaul of Our Terms & Licensing
>This month marks a foundational shift in our philosophy. To better empower our community and accelerate development, we've completely revised our legal and distribution framework.
>
>**Now MIT, Always MIT:** The complex, time-based licensing model from our initial release has been completely removed. All BETO.GROUP software is now, and will forever be, licensed under the simple and permissive MIT License.
>
>**From Paywall to Public:** After consulting with our team of experts, we concluded that the best path forward for the community and the project's longevity is to move away from a paywall model. We are officially releasing the entire Obsidian Vault on GitHub. This lowers the barrier to entry, makes it easier for others to contribute, and will ultimately speed up future development cycles.
>
>You can explore, fork, and contribute to the project on our [official GitHub repository](https://github.com/beto-group/BETO.888).
>
>**"Reciprocity Ethos":** In line with this new openness, the legally binding "Give Back Mechanism" has been replaced by a non-binding, good-faith community principle that encourages voluntary contributions. No mandatory obligations.
>
>All related documents have been updated to reflect this new, simpler, and more open approach.

#### II. New Creative Arsenal: Datacore Components

We've added a powerful suite of new tools to the vault to expand your creative capabilities.

##### New Additions

- **LOADSCRIPT:** A new utility for dynamically loading scripts from CDNs.
- **Fuzzy Text & Matrix Glitch Wall:** Components for creating visually interesting text and background effects.
- **Loading Logo, Sound Player, Music Player:** A suite of new media and branding components.
- **CodeEditor (v1 + v2):** Two versions of an in-vault code editor for writing and testing snippets.
- **Animated Card:** Create cards that flip or animate on click.
- **ActivityWatch Dashboard:** A dashboard for integrating with the ActivityWatch time-tracking application.

##### Major Upgrades

- **IframePlayer:** The Iframe loader has been significantly upgraded and renamed.
- **Window Resizer with Tab Mode:** Now includes a powerful Tab Mode, allowing you to embed multiple windows within a single, tabbed component.

#### III. Developer's Log: The Godot Experiment

This month, we also embarked on a side quest: building a game! Our initial dream was to have it run directly inside Obsidian utilizing Datacore, but we discovered that running a Godot game in Obsidian without a dedicated server isn't feasible. Still, it was an incredible learning experience. We dove into GIMP to create sprites from scratch and got our first real hands-on experience with the Godot engine, which was surprisingly easy to pick up.

While it couldn't live in the vault, we're proud of the result. [You can play our first game, "Find My Love," on itch.io!](https://betogroup.itch.io/find-my-love)

#### IV. A Shift in Web Strategy

This month's experiments have highlighted a critical insight. Given the evolving landscape and the pace of development for web-facing features within the Obsidian ecosystem, we have made the strategic decision to begin development on our own bespoke web solutions. This shift will allow us to create more powerful, integrated, and reliable experiences for our community, independent of external platform limitations. We are officially rethinking our approach and building for the future.
