January/December is a time for resetting. I’ve been reviewing the recap of the past year, and it is genuinely exciting to see the progress we’ve made. It’s amazing that we managed to build so much while constantly traveling and figuring things out on the fly.

Currently, I’ve been working on several backend components. Many are still unpolished or are private tools I use to manage our infrastructure. Slowly but surely, I am revisiting our entire infrastructure stack to ensure it can handle everything we have planned for the future, with user privacy remaining the absolute top priority.

For this new year, our main goals are defined by three pillars:

- **Sovereignty**
- **Self-Sufficiency**
- **Health 2.0**


It’s going to be a busy year. "Health 2.0" involves a complete revisit of how we approach well-being, including designing a custom methodology and principles to follow.

**Project Updates:**

- **BETO.888 Vault:** We are redesigning the Vault to focus on offering experiences rather than functioning simply as a Datacore Library. Now that the Marketplace is live, it makes sense to transition the Vault to a more streamlined design.
    
- **The Recap Component:** Designing the yearly recap was a fun challenge. We utilized Datacore Obsidian for the animations and styling, completely removing the need for external video tools. We plan to post this component soon so you can review the code and see how it works.


The next version will offer pure exploration, revisiting the main reason we started all of this in the first place. but for now relaxing and keeping it easy until relocating again. 

We'll be around. 🫡
