
### Preserving the Core, Unleashing the Tab

Our relationship with Obsidian is one of respect and enhancement. We do not seek to change what makes it great; we seek to build upon it.

The original functionality of your vault is sacred and remains untouched. Instead, we see each tab as a potential portal. When you activate a component, that single tab becomes a self-contained universe—a canvas for a new kind of experience.

We believe an interface should do more than present data; it should **narrate a journey**. By giving ourselves the freedom to design any interface we desire, we can build guided, immersive worlds that help you think, create, and explore in ways a simple document never could. This is how we break free from the page and step into a space limited only by imagination.
