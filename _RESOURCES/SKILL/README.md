### 5. [[knowledge/|Manifestos]] (The Expertise)
High-density thematic documents consolidating years of specific engineering, design, and logic experience (e.g., `obsidian-dev-elite`).

### 6. [[roles/|Personas]] & [[agency/|Operations]]
- **Roles**: Core engineering personas (Architect, Senior Developer).
- **Agency**: Specialized departmental logic (Marketing, Product, Distribution).

### 7. [[interface/|Interface]] & [[templates/|Templates]]
The visual and structural blueprints for the **Digital Factotums** and interactive experiences built within the vault.

### 8. [[memory/|Persistence]]
- **active/**: Volatile session state for real-time agent rehydration.
